Where We Begin

Everything Here Exists for a Reason

People usually do not come through my door because they need a particular modality. They come because something in their life is asking for attention. A relationship is changing. Grief has cracked something open. The same pattern keeps repeating. Their intuition is getting louder. Their body is carrying something they cannot quite name. They are changing and the life around them has not caught up yet. Or they simply know there is more to understand about themselves.

That is where we begin.

I work with a lot of different tools — intuitive guidance, channeling, energy healing, breathwork, ancestral work, astrology and Soul Blueprint, crystals, teaching, and sometimes my own lived experience. I do not expect every person to need all of them. They are simply different ways of getting closer to what is actually happening for you.

Some people need one conversation. Some need a map. Some need a year of work. Some need community. Some need to lie on the floor and breathe. Some need to walk through the woods and remember they have a body. There is no single correct doorway here.

The purpose of all of it is the same: to help you understand what is happening in your life, recognize what belongs to you and what does not, reconnect with your own knowing, and become more able to live as yourself.

I have many ways of working because people do not arrive in the same place, and they do not need the same thing.

Brain Leeches

Brain Leeches is my name for the beliefs that lodge so deeply in us that they begin to sound like our own voice. They can come through family, religion, culture, trauma, relationships, and the systems we grew up inside. Some may be part of the particular curriculum a soul came here to encounter. Others are picked up simply by being human in the world we entered.

The work is learning to recognize them: Is this actually mine? Do I believe this? Did I choose this? Or have I been living inside something I inherited?

Your Soul Blueprint helps identify the patterns most active in your life, so you can begin separating what shaped you from what is actually you.
