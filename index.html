Kristin Wolf is an author, teacher, depth and transformation coach, and founder of Magic Mushrooms in the Crystal Garden. Her work combines soul cartography, ceremony, ancestral healing, breathwork, and community-building to help people understand themselves and the world they live in.

Kristin Wolf is a channeler, author, teacher, and crystal energy healing master based in Zimmerman, Minnesota. She spent decades leading people and developing leaders — and for the last decade has been pursuing the healing arts with the same intensity. She sits with people in the hardest moments of their lives, reads what their soul came here to do, and tells them the truth about what she sees. Based in Zimmerman, Minnesota, she works with people locally and remotely across the country.

She works with people one on one through the SOUL Blueprint — a six-month soul cartography journey built from your birth chart, written specifically for you. She runs year-long containers through the Spark. She leads retreats on sacred land. She runs a crystal shop out of her home in Zimmerman. And she writes — books, transmissions, witness work — because some things need to be said out loud.

Her work is rooted in lived experience. She is a grandmother, a survivor, a political activist, and a teacher who did not come to repeat what she read. She came to say what she survived, remembered, and was sent to say. It's all in her Soul Blueprint.

Everything here started with losing her mother. Nancy Adams died February 8, 2023. My Mama's a Peacock was written in the six months that followed. It is about mothers and daughters, family, and love. About what you realize you lost once it's gone and how that changes the way you show up for everyone still here. It is also about how to stay connected — how spirit speaks through signs, symbols, and synchronicity after someone crosses. The language of the other side. Nancy still shows up. Kristin still listens. That is where all of this began.

About This Work

Kristin Wolf spent decades running multi-million dollar retail operations for Walmart, Sam's Club, and JCPenney. She managed people, budgets, systems, and culture at scale. She knows what it takes to build something that works — and she knows that most small business owners never get access to the tools that make it possible.

AI changed that. A professional web developer, copywriter, and content strategist would have charged $5,000–$12,000 to build what Kristin built using AI. She built it herself. On her phone. With no technical background. In the same weeks she was running retreats, finishing books, and building a healing practice.

AI is a field leveler. For the first time in history, a small business owner can have the same quality digital presence as a corporation with a marketing budget. But only if you know how to use it — and how to hold it accountable. That's what this container teaches.

The Container — 5 Months

Month 1 — Your Brand & Voice

Who you are, what you do, who you serve. Your story. Your differentiator. We begin by teaching Claude who you are — so everything it helps you build sounds like you, not a machine.

Month 2 — Your Website

We build it together. You direct. AI executes. You walk away with a real website you built yourself and own completely.

Month 3 — Your Content

Substack, social, email. How to use AI to write without losing your voice. How to show up consistently without burning out.

Month 4 — Your Business Systems

Booking, scheduling, payments. Getting your back end as clean as your front end. Making sure everything connects.

Month 5 — AI Literacy & Accountability

How to verify. How to push back. How to know when it's lying. How to use it as a tool without becoming dependent on it.
